Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Easy Wash ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding user privacy and maintaining absolute security across our public washroom network.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential registration credentials (such as verified emails and mobile numbers) along with location details (PIN codes, addresses, and GPS pinpoints) strictly for location-based distance matching and administrative listing management.

2. Location Data & GPS Usage

Device or search location coordinates are processed dynamically to calculate 20 km search radiuses. We do not store persistent tracking histories.

3. Zero-Surveillance Guarantee

We enforce a strict anti-surveillance policy. Installing cameras or monitoring devices inside washrooms is strictly forbidden and subject to immediate legal action and criminal reporting.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted mapping components (Leaflet.js & OpenStreetMap Nominatim) for location plotting without selling or disclosing personal identifiers to advertisers.
